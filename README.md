## 🚀 Overview

**Shorts Bar** transforms how you interact with your Windows desktop. Instead of cluttering your screen with scattered shortcuts, you can organize your favorite programs, folders, and tools into sleek, **custom-styled floating bars** and expandable groups.

Designed to be **fast, seamless, and genuinely satisfying**, Shorts Bar blends smooth animations, modern visual themes (including **Aero Glass**), and live hardware monitoring into one lightweight utility.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🪟 **Floating & Expandable Bars:** Create multiple floating docks anywhere on your screen with smooth expand/collapse animations.
- 🎨 **Deep Visual Customization:** Personalize colors, transparency, blur/Aero themes, borders, and hover effects to match any wallpaper or setup.
- 📁 **Smart Groups & Quick Access:** Group your most-used apps, folders, and shortcuts into clean, instant-access menus.
- 📊 **Live System Monitoring:** Keep an eye on your PC with built-in, stylish graphs for **CPU**, **RAM (Memory)**, and **Disk** usage right inside your bar.
- ⚡ **Lightweight & Portable:** Runs directly from its extracted folder with no bloated installers slowing down your system.
- 🌍 **Multi-Language Support:** Easily switch or customize interface languages using external `.lang` files.
